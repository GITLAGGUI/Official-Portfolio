# SEO with evidence

Identify the intended website, its public pages and the business task search should support. Inspect existing project context and reports before starting another crawl or paid research run.

Trace titles, descriptions, canonicals, robots, sitemap and structured data in the actual application. Distinguish public discovery pages from private workspace, account and administration routes. Check the rendered output as well as the source configuration.

Use Search Console for first-party search and indexing evidence. Record the property, date range and available dimensions. Preserve zeros, missing data and processing states. A dashboard capture proves tool use and a dated observation; it does not by itself prove growth or causation.

Read completed audit findings in context. Prioritize issues that affect discovery, usability or a meaningful customer action. Recheck a changed issue in the page or response that produced it.

Keep credentials, account settings and unrelated customer data out of public evidence. Deliver source-backed findings, focused changes and dated captures or reports. Separate observed outcomes from hypotheses and any measurements that still need time or access.
