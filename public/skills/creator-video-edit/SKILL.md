# Creator video edit

Inspect the supplied recording and confirm the intended style, audience and output format when those choices are missing. Keep the source footage intact.

Use the actual speech as the edit clock. Remove dead air, false starts and repeated takes without cutting away meaning or making the speaker sound unnatural. Review cuts in context instead of trusting silence thresholds alone.

Let visuals explain what the speaker is saying. Prefer real product captures, footage and source-backed examples. Retain the speaker's real background unless the chosen direction calls for another treatment. Keep the face clear when a graphic shares the frame.

Match captions to the final audio. Check names, timing, phrase breaks and platform overlay areas. Keep text readable on a phone; do not fill the frame with persistent headings. Add sound effects only where they support a visible action or a meaningful beat.

Check voice intelligibility, music balance, caption spelling, picture continuity, final dimensions and full-file decoding. Watch the complete encoded output. Deliver the finished video and the editable project, with any missing asset or unresolved audio issue identified.
